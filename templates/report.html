Reading a dot

One dot is one posting. Further right means it asks for more of what your record shows. Yellow dots are in your top 10% and you can be hired into them. Hollow dots ask for more years or a higher level than you have.

Sample size

100+ postings in a row: trust the pattern. 40 to 99: a direction. Under 40 is marked thin: read the roles one by one.

What fit is not

Fit is word overlap with your evidence bank, ranked within this sweep. It compares rows on this page. It doesn't compare you with anyone else, and it isn't a hiring prediction.
